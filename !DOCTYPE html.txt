      <h2>Choosing the Right Digital Learning Resource</h2>

      <p>
        There is no single educational platform that fits every learner or classroom. A useful approach is to begin with the learning objective, then consider the student's age, subject, desired level of interaction, available technology, and type of feedback required. Educational games can support engagement, while quizzes, presentations, assessment tools, and guided resources can serve different parts of the learning process.
      </p>

      <p>
        By combining purposeful instruction with appropriate digital resources, educators can create learning experiences that are interactive without losing sight of educational goals. Students can use these resources to practice, review, explore new concepts, and develop confidence through active participation.
